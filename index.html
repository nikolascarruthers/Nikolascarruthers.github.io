What I built this fall

I built ParkGuard because I kept getting parking tickets.

I live on Mission Blvd, which gets more street sweeping tickets than any other street in San Diego. I have six roommates and not everyone fits in the driveway, so the tickets added up. I wanted an app that would just remind me, so I made one.

01 · The idea

I wanted something that would just remind me.

The idea was simple. You park, walk away, and get a reminder before the street sweeper comes, without ever opening the app. It saves your spot on its own when your phone disconnects from the car.

02 · The build

Building it took about six weeks.

I'm not a CS major, so a lot of this was new to me. I planned out every feature, built it in Swift, and tested it constantly, including driving around San Diego to find bugs. One early version dropped my pin on the freeway, so that got fixed fast. It passed App Review with 48,000+ lines of code and 1,300+ automated tests.

03 · The launch

Then I had to get people to actually use it.

I went through 494,000+ San Diego parking tickets to figure out which neighborhoods get hit the hardest. Then I built a tool in Python that makes TikTok posts for me, and made QR fliers with their own tracking links so I could see what was working.
